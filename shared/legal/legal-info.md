## User Safety

Your safety is important to us. We recommend:

- **Housing**: Never send payment before viewing a property; verify landlord identity
- **Roommates**: Meet potential roommates in public first; trust your instincts
- **Marketplace**: Meet in public, well-lit areas; bring someone with you for high-value items
- **Communication**: Keep conversations on-platform; report harassment immediately
